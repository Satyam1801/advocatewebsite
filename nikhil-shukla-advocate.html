About

A focused practice in cyber and technology law

Nikhil Shukla is an advocate practicing before the Delhi High Court, with a focused practice in cyber crime, information technology law and digital evidence. His work spans anticipatory bail and quashing petitions under the IT Act, data breach and privacy disputes, online financial fraud, cyber defamation and matters involving electronic evidence under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam / Evidence Act.

He regularly advises corporates on compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and data protection obligations, and appears before the Delhi Police Cyber Cell, trial courts and the Delhi High Court.
